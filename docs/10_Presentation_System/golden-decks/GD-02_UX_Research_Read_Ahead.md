# GD-02 — UX / Research Read-ahead

> Status: Planned  
> System Version: 0.9.0

## Brief

Create a self-guided UX or research deck that preserves evidence, sources, findings, implications, and recommendations.

## Required Structure

1. Executive summary
2. Research question and scope
3. Method
4. Evidence
5. Findings
6. Implications
7. Recommendations
8. Limitations
9. Appendix and sources

## Validation Focus

- Independent comprehension
- Evidence traceability
- Dense content readability
- Footnotes and source behavior
- Inclusive and precise language

## Required Review

- Release Gates
- Risk-based Audit
